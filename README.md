## Paradigms, Systems and Benchmarks

_Baseline (TIGER), end-to-end multi-stage paradigms, open-source frameworks, benchmarks, and diagnostic resources._

- [TIGER: Recommender Systems with Generative Retrieval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.05065) - Rajput et al. (NeurIPS’23, [Page](pages/papers/tiger.md), [Code](https://github.com/baiyimeng/TIGER)) (`Annotated Deep Dive`). `Rep: Text` · `Tok: RQ-VAE` · `Backbone: Enc-Dec` · `Train: Fixed / NTP` · `Inf: Beam`. Encodes item text into hierarchical RQ-VAE Semantic IDs and autoregressively decodes target item identifiers with a T5 encoder-decoder.
- [FORGE: Forge: Forming semantic identifiers for generative retrieval in industrial datasets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.20904) - Fu et al. (KDD’26, [Page](pages/papers/forge.md), [Code](https://huggingface.co/AL-GR)). `Rep: Text+Img` · `Tok: RQ-VAE` · `Backbone: Dec-Only` · `Train: Fixed / NTP` · `Inf: Dyn. Beam`. Abstract: "their SID assessment typically relies on costly GR training".
- [GRID: GRID: Generative Recommendation via Inpainting Diffusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.22224) - Ju et al. (CIKM’25, [Page](pages/papers/grid.md)). `Rep: Text` · `Tok: R-KM` · `Backbone: Enc-Dec` · `Train: Fixed / NTP` · `Inf: Beam`. Abstract: "the absence of an open-source, unified framework hinders systematic benchmarking and extension".
- [MiniOneRec: MiniOneRec: An Open-Source Framework for Scaling Generative Recommendation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24431) - Kong et al. (arXiv’25, [Page](pages/papers/minionerec.md), [Code](https://github.com/AkaliKong/MiniOneRec)). `Rep: Text` · `Tok: RQ-VAE` · `Backbone: Dec-Only` · `Train: Fixed / NTP → RL` · `Inf: Beam`. Abstract: "most industrial deployments remain proprietary".
- [OneRec: Onerec: Unifying retrieve and rank with generative recommender and iterative preference alignment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.18965) - Deng et al. (arXiv’25, [Page](pages/papers/onerec.md)). `Rep: Text+CF` · `Tok: R-KM` · `Backbone: Enc-Dec` · `Train: Fixed / NTP → DPO` · `Inf: Sess. Beam`. Abstract: "the generative model functions only as a selector during the retrieval stage".

(arXiv’26, [Page](pages/papers/gr-generalize.md)). `Rep: Text+SID` · `Tok: RQ-VAE` · `Backbone: Enc-Dec` · `Train: Fixed† / NTP†` · `Inf: Hybrid`. Abstract: "there is few systematic way to verify this hypothesis".

End-to-end paradigm or benchmark using R-KM tokenization and Dec-Only backbone.

RL after SFT` · `Inf: C-Beam / Scoring`. End-to-end paradigm or benchmark using RQ-VAE tokenization and Dec-Only / Non-AR backbone.
- [OneMall: OneMall: One Architecture, More Scenarios -- End-to-End Generative Recommender Family at Kuaishou E-Commerce](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.21770) - Zhang et al. End-to-end paradigm or benchmark using R-KM+FSQ tokenization and Enc-Dec backbone.

## I. Informativeness

_Do the SIDs, or the model reading them, carry the collaborative and multimodal signals recommendation needs?_

### I Representation

- [Molar: Molar: Multimodal llms with collaborative filtering alignment for enhanced sequential recommendation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.18176) - Luo et al. (arXiv’24, [Page](pages/papers/molar.md)). `Rep: Text+Img+CF` · `Tok: –` · `Backbone: Dec-Only` · `Train: – / Discrim.` · `Inf: Scoring`. Abstract: "inherently lack collaborative filtering information, relying primarily on textual content data neglecting other modalities".
- [OneLoc: OneLoc: Geo-Aware Generative Recommender Systems for Local Life Service](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.14646) - Wei et al. (arXiv’25 `POI`, [Page](pages/papers/oneloc.md)) (`Annotated Deep Dive`). `Rep: Text+Img+Geo†` · `Tok: R-KM` · `Backbone: Enc-Dec` · `Train: Fixed / NTP → DPO` · `Inf: Beam`. Abstract: "how to make full use of geographic information"; "how to balance multiple objectives, including user interests, the distance between user and stores, and some other business objectives". Three designs fix I (geo-aware SID, geo-aware self-attention, neighbor-aware prompt) against two rewards for IV. The complaints fit the six problems, so the paper is not in the top block (unlike OneRec).
- [Q-BERT4Rec: Q-BERT4Rec: Quantized Semantic-ID Representation Learning for Multimodal Recommendation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.02474) - Huang and Gai (arXiv’25, [Page](pages/papers/q-bert4rec.md)). `Rep: Text+Img+CF` · `Tok: RQ-VAE` · `Backbone: Encoder (Non-AR)` · `Train: Fixed / Masked` · `Inf: Non-AR`. Abstract: "rely on discrete item IDs that lack semantic meaning and ignore rich multimodal information".
- [UNGER: UNGER: Generative Recommendation with A Unified Code via Semantic and Collaborative Integration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.06269) - Xiao et al. (TOIS’25, [Page](pages/papers/unger.md)). `Rep: Text+CF` · `Tok: R-KM` · `Backbone: Enc-Dec` · `Train: Fixed / NTP + Ctr.` · `Inf: Beam`. 提出统一表示、离散编码及完整 Unicode 生成查表流程。.
- [GeoGR: GeoGR: Enabling Spatio-Temporal Aware Industrial-Scale Generative POI Recommendations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.10411) - Wang et al. (arXiv’26 `POI`, [Page](pages/papers/geogr.md)). `Rep: Text+Geo+CF` · `Tok: R-KM+EM` · `Backbone: Dec-Only` · `Train: Altern.† / Multi-task NTP` · `Inf: Beam`. Abstract: "inadequate modeling of high-quality SIDs that capture cross-category spatio-temporal collaborative relationships"; "poor alignment between large language models (LLMs) and the POI recommendation task". I and III tie, and so do the new designs; the title ("Enabling Spatio-Temporal Aware ...") names I.
- [HF-SID: HF-SID: High-Fidelity Semantic IDs for Generative Retrieval in Location-Based Services](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30479) - Lin et al. (arXiv’26 `POI`, [Page](pages/papers/hf-sid.md)). `Rep: Text+Attr+Geo` · `Tok: R-KM` · `Backbone: Dec-Only` · `Train: Fixed / NTP` · `Inf: C-Beam`. 直接研究 POI SID 质量并用于下游 next-POI 生成，建议保留方法；完整码到多 POI 的物化仍须明确标未知。.
- [LGRID: Interpretable Representation via LLM-Driven Generative Disentanglement for Local-Life Service Recommendation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27944) - Zhang et al. (arXiv’26 `POI`, [Page](pages/papers/lgrid.md)). `Rep: Text+Geo` · `Tok: DSRQ` · `Backbone: Disc.` · `Train: Fixed / Discrim.` · `Inf: Scoring`. Abstract: "semantic entanglement ... causing key attributes to be insufficiently preserved during quantization, degrading SID quality, and leading to severe SID collisions". Collisions are the consequence; the cause is lost key attributes (I). The interpretability complaint fits none of the six problems.
- [LGSID: LLM-Aligned Geographic Item Tokenization for Local-Life Recommendation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.14221) - Jiang et al. (AAAI’26 `POI`, [Page](pages/papers/lgsid.md)). `Rep: Text+Geo+CF` · `Tok: Geo-Idx+RQ` · `Backbone: Disc. / Enc-Dec` · `Train: Fixed / Discrim. / NTP` · `Inf: Scoring / Beam†`. Abstract: "simply injecting location information into prompts fails to capture fine-grained spatial characteristics and real-world distance awareness among items".
- [ReSID: Rethinking Generative Recommender Tokenizer: Recsys-Native Encoding and Semantic Quantization Beyond LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02338) - Liang et al. (arXiv’26, [Page](pages/papers/resid.md)). `Rep: Attr+CF` · `Tok: GAOQ` · `Backbone: Enc-Dec` · `Train: Fixed / NTP` · `Inf: Beam`. 提出完整 SID 构造方法并在统一生成推荐器上验证。.
- [SCRec: Addressing Cross-Stage Decoupling of Semantic and Collaborative Signals in Generative Recommendation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13678) - Dan (RecSys’26, [Page](pages/papers/screc.md)). `Rep: Text+CF` · `Tok: RQ-VAE` · `Backbone: Non-AR / Enc-Dec` · `Train: Fixed / MTP + Ctr.` · `Inf: Scoring / Beam†`. Abstract: "item tokenization is largely dominated by textual semantics with limited incorporation of collaborative signals"; "the generation stage tends to overlook the original semantic information".
- [SPAR: SPAR: Enhancing Industrial-Scale Generative POI Recommendation via Real-World Spatial Perception](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02062) - Wang et al. (arXiv’26 `POI`, [Page](pages/papers/spar.md)). `Rep: Text+Geo` · `Tok: R-KM` · `Backbone: Dec-Only` · `Train: Fixed / Multi-task NTP` · `Inf: –`. 完整四位 SI-SID 含显式消歧码，三项空间机制独立可定位。.
- [TriAlignGR: TriAlignGR: Triangular Multitask Alignment with Multimodal Deep Interest Mining for Generative Recommendation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05249) - Zeng et al. (arXiv’26, [Page](pages/papers/trialigngr.md)). `Rep: Text+Img` · `Tok: RQ-VAE` · `Backbone: Dec-Only` · `Train: Fixed / Multi-task NTP` · `Inf: C-Beam`. Abstract: "SID Content Degradation (SCD)"; "SID Semantic Opacity (SSO)". Candidates: I / III (rule: tie broken by the majority of new designs).
